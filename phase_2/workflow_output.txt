Task Title: Establish Regular Meeting Schedule with Development Team

Related User Story: As a project manager, I want to schedule regular meetings with the development team to ensure effective communication and alignment towards successful product delivery.

Description: The task involves setting up a regular meeting schedule with the development team to facilitate communication, address any challenges, provide support, and ensure everyone is aligned towards the successful delivery of the product. Schedule weekly meetings on Mondays at 10:00 AM. Include all development team members, the project manager, and relevant stakeholders. Cover project updates, challenges faced, action items, and other pertinent topics in the agenda. Document and share meeting minutes with all participants after each meeting.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. Weekly meetings scheduled on Mondays at 10:00 AM.
2. All development team members, project manager, and relevant stakeholders are included in the meetings.
3. Meeting agenda covers project updates, challenges, action items, and other pertinent topics.
4. Meeting minutes are documented and shared with all participants after each meeting.

Estimated Effort: 2 hours per week for meeting preparation, facilitation, and documentation.

Dependencies: Availability of all team members and stakeholders for the scheduled meeting times.
